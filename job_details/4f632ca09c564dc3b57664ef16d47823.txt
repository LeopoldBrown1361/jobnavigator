**Job Title:** Pricing Analyst Intern
**Location:** Knoxville, TN, US
**Requisition ID:** 1290

**Job Purpose & Responsibilities:**
- Assist in analyzing and preparing pricing models for products and services, including global pricing strategies and small project pricing reviews.
- Support the pricing team in evaluating market trends, competitive pricing data, and insights from Inside Sales Quote reviews.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including sales, finance, and product management, to gather data and provide insights that support pricing decisions.
- Smartly leverage information and data to create a continuous learning process, build upon results, and drive improvements in pricing strategies.
- Perform ad-hoc analytical tasks as required by the pricing team, including adjustments based on feedback from global pricing assessments and internal review processes.
- Other reasonable directives, responsibilities, and activities may change or be assigned to meet business needs/requirements.
- Leverage business tools and platforms, such as ERP systems, CRM software, data visualization tools, and pricing analytics software, to improve accuracy and efficiency in decision-making and reporting.

**Minimum Qualifications:**
- Currently pursuing a degree in Business, Analytics, Economics, Finance, or a related field.
- Strong analytical skills with attention to detail and accuracy.
- Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, with experience in data analysis and reporting.
- Ability to work independently and in a team-oriented environment.
- Strong communication skills, both written and verbal.
- Eagerness to learn and develop new skills in pricing and analytics.
- Prior internship experience or coursework related to pricing or data analysis is a plus.

**Preferred Qualifications:**
- Power BI experience
- Salesforce experience
- Advanced Excel Skills

**Physical Demands:**
- Ability to lift up to OSHA single-person lift requirements.
- Ability to sit, stand, bend, or walk for prolonged periods of time.
- Must be able to work a hybrid schedule, in the Knoxville office on Tuesdays and Wednesdays.
